# ISE Domain design direction

Owner direction: Keep ISE Domain's Japanese industry identity, with stronger typography and calmer surfaces.

Reading this as: a bilingual field guide for engineering students, using an industrial editorial language. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1.

## Identity and hierarchy

- Retain the name, Japanese domain mark, original subject illustrations, Japanese manufacturing principles, and English/Persian content: these already connect the guide to its subject.
- Use ink, paper, and a restrained vermilion accent drawn from the Japanese seal motif. The accent identifies primary actions, current selections, and focus, rather than decorating every surface.
- Retain Barlow Condensed for large editorial headings: its compact proportions fit engineering publication titles. Use Manrope for Latin reading text and Sahel for Persian, with Vazirmatn Persian numerals.
- Keep both themes and existing saved preferences. Charcoal supports the existing dark reading preference; the light theme uses warm paper for longer reading sessions.
- Lead with the curriculum and real connections to careers. Replace abstract promises with descriptions of what the guide contains.
- Present the seven subject categories as a numbered index: equal treatment communicates taxonomy. Use compact navigation rows for the supporting sections instead of another feature-card grid.
- Use the original systems illustration as a separate hero figure, with reserved dimensions. Subject thumbnails identify their curriculum topic; grayscale treatment prevents seven competing accent colors.
- Retain filled, bordered Career Guide domain groups because they communicate real parent/child relationships, including 1.5 Industrial/Manufacturing.
- Use structural whitespace: a generous introduction, a denser course index, a compact directory, and a distinct Japanese-industry reading panel.

## Interaction and surfaces

- Motion 1: hover/focus feedback only. No automatic carousel, scroll entrance effects, orbit loops, pulsing glows, or theme wipe. The data-science combination remains a user-triggered state change.
- Solid surfaces and 4/8px radii distinguish controls and containers. Shadows are reserved for floating menus and dialogs; no glass layers or glow.
- Keep relevant utility icons for search, language, theme, filtering, dialog close, and external destinations. The GitHub star represents the actual repository star count. Directory links use text and numbering rather than decorative icons.
- Arrows are reserved for external destinations and explicit next/previous controls.
- Preserve URL-backed filters, all course/career dialogs, the Mathematics hierarchy, MR/PP ordering, resources, and all existing routes.
- Controls have visible labels or accessible names, 44px minimum touch targets, a visible focus indicator, and meaningful empty/loading/error states.
- No new logo, illustration, photography, or invented metrics. Totals come from the catalog and official degree requirements.
- The degree-credit bar retains distinct segment colors to identify official course groups. These are categorical data encodings, not additional brand accents.
